Featured Narrative

Light in the classroom: Rebuilding learning post-disruption

In a small village near Cuttack, 12-year-old Puja had fallen two grade levels behind during pandemic disruptions. Her parents, daily wage earners, couldn't afford private tutoring.

Through Sattvic Soul Foundation's evening remedial learning center, Puja received daily guided instruction, digital tablet lessons, and a complete school kit. Today, she tops her class in mathematics and dreams of becoming an environmental scientist.
