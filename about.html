Our Story & Mission

Founded on September 10, 2019 in Bhubaneswar, Odisha, Sattvic Soul Foundation is dedicated to bringing lasting revival and joy to underserved communities.

Origin & Purpose

Sanjeevani for a Little Smile

Sattvic Soul Foundation was established by Mr. Satyabrata Samal alongside a team of committed change-makers to bridge developmental gaps across Odisha and 13+ states in India.

The word Sanjeevani signifies renewal, healing, and life-giving hope. We believe that true social progress is achieved not through transient charity, but by co-creating sustainable systems with local communities. Our work focuses on four interconnected pillars: high-impact education, environmental stewardship, accessible healthcare, and economic empowerment for women.

Our Journey

Milestones & Growth

From a local grassroots effort in Bhubaneswar to a multi-district organization impacting thousands of lives.

September 2019

Foundation Established

Registered under Section 8 of the Companies Act, 2013 in Bhubaneswar, starting with immediate scholarship and learning kit support for 120 children in Khordha district.

2020 – 2021

Healthcare & Relief Expansion

Launched free health checkup camps and maternal care kits in rural Odisha villages, while scaling tree plantation drives across 5 coastal districts.

2022 – 2023

Women Livelihood Cooperatives

Established tailoring and micro-entrepreneurship training centers in Mayurbhanj, Cuttack, and Koraput, creating self-sustaining cooperatives for 300+ women.

2024 – Present

National Footprint & SDG Alignment

Expanded programs across 13+ states and UTs in India while maintaining deep operational headquarters in Bhubaneswar, Odisha.
