Our Mission

Environmental Stewardship & Community Empowerment

Rooted in Bhubaneswar, Sattvic Soul Foundation was born from a singular belief: protecting our environment and empowering communities creates lasting change.

We operate as a Section 8 non-profit organization dedicated to sustainable, community-owned growth. From large-scale tree plantation drives and plastic-free village campaigns to essential healthcare and women's livelihood programs, we build long-term solutions that protect our planet and uplift rural Odisha.

Zero-overhead model

Driving Environmental Change & Community Growth

Environmental Care

Native tree plantation drives, plastic-free village campaigns, community cleaning initiatives, rainwater harvesting, and waste management programs.
